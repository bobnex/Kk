Do I need an appointment?

An appointment is recommended so we can understand your concern and plan the visit. Submit a request and we'll contact you to confirm availability.

Does a diagnostic code always mean a part has failed?

No. A diagnostic code is a clue that helps guide further checks. It should be interpreted alongside symptoms and relevant system tests.

Can I request a service if I'm unsure what is wrong?

Yes. Describe what you have noticed, including when it happens. We can discuss an appropriate assessment.

Is my online appointment automatically confirmed?

No. The form sends an enquiry. The workshop will contact you to confirm availability and any next steps.

Where are you located?
